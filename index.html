how it began

Our Story

chapter one

How We Met

It started at the Nest School in Takoradi, where Dag — then 10 — and Gayle — then 9 — were classmates who barely knew each other. Life carried them along separate paths until 2015, when a 15-year-old Dag crossed paths with Gayle again.

Years later, they found themselves reconnecting once more, this time at a radio show. What began as friendship quickly grew close, and somewhere along the way, close friends became something more.

chapter two

The Proposal

On 12th December 2024, Dag showed up at Gayle's house — and asked her to marry him. She said yes. The two of them went out to eat that evening to celebrate the beginning of their forever.

"From classmates at the Nest School Complex to forever — some stories just take the scenic route."
